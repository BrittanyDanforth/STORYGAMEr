# Character reference and acceptance notes — v0.9

The police character pass uses the project's own Blender geometry, materials, skeletons, and animation code. No downloaded character, rig, add-on, texture pack, or executable was imported for this pass. These public pages are visual references, not asset sources or instructions to run software.

## What the correction must demonstrate

- The upper arm leaves the shoulder laterally and forward without disappearing into the sternum or carrier. A small elbow bend remains visible where the pose calls for it.
- Shoulder seams can join the torso naturally. That seam is not permission to exempt the rest of the upper sleeve from intersection review.
- Sleeve weights follow shoulder and arm motion without a star-shaped armpit collapse or a chest-driven strip crossing the body.
- Wrist and finger corrections survive the shoulder change: stable grip orientation, opposed thumbs, wrapped fingers, and no sudden hand roll through transitions.
- Gear has an intentional silhouette and attachment. Belt items follow the pelvis; chest equipment follows the torso; a stowed prop belongs visibly inside its holster.
- Front, side, rear, and overhead views must agree. Full-frame movement and close-up review are both required; a favorable still or joint-angle metric is insufficient.
- Exports must be read back. Blender inspection does not establish Unity playback, first-person clipping, or GTX 1080 Ti performance.

The checks reduce specific, observed risks. They are not a guarantee that any software or asset is free from every possible vulnerability. Keep Blender auto-execution disabled during verification; inspect embedded scripts, drivers, external links, source hashes, and the unchanged optional-AI network boundary separately from visual quality.
